## New components

- [DonutChart](./?path=/docs/charts-donutchart--docs) and
  [LineChart](./?path=/docs/charts-linechart--docs) draw with
  `react-native-svg`. A read-only LineChart is one labelled image; with
  `onSelect` it becomes a group of point buttons.
- [SelectionFeedback](./?path=/docs/feedback-selectionfeedback--docs) pops a
  deliberate selection. Calendar opts in with `selectionAnimation`; RadioButton
  uses it by default. The system Reduce Motion setting always wins.
- [TimePicker](./?path=/docs/inputs-timepicker--docs) writes hours and minutes
  the way a given locale does, independent of the device language.
- [Calendar](./?path=/docs/inputs-calendar--docs),
  [ParallaxHeader](./?path=/docs/display-parallaxheader--docs),
  [PageIndicator](./?path=/docs/feedback-pageindicator--docs) and
  [ReorderableList](./?path=/docs/display-reorderablelist--docs) arrived during
  the betas.

## Behavior changes

- Typography keeps Korean words whole on iOS (`lineBreakStrategyIOS:
  'hangul-word'`).
- AlertDialog avoids the Android keyboard and can be controlled with `visible`
  and `onClose`.
- Text buttons pad `8px 16px` and hug their label.

## Upgrading from 0.7

1. Install the new peers: `react-native-svg`, `react-native-worklets` and
   `@shopify/flash-list`, with React 19 and Reanimated 4.
2. Pass `selectionAnimation={false}` to RadioButton if it should stay still.
3. Keep `borderRadius` on Button as a prop, never in `style`.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/crossplatformkorea/cpk-ui/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
